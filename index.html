Who we are

Free social tennis in Didsbury

About us

Community-run tennis organised by volunteers who give their time freely, supported by McrActive to provide accessible tennis for Didsbury and South Manchester. An inclusive, friendly environment where fun and social connection are at the heart of every session. As a volunteer group we don't have coaches on hand, so we're best suited to players who are already comfortable with the basics.

Sociable & inclusive

Warm, friendly atmosphere where everyone's welcome. Random partnering keeps things fresh and helps you meet new people.

Beautiful location

Set in the lovely surroundings of Fletcher Moss Park. A great way to exercise, unwind, and enjoy the outdoors.

No fees or contracts

No membership fees or contracts. Just a small contribution towards balls every few months to keep things running.

Your first session

Just turn up, introduce yourself and you'll be paired up straight away. We rotate partners throughout so you'll get to play with everyone. Bring water, wear trainers, and expect a good time. There's no coaching at our sessions, so a few lessons beforehand will help you settle in and enjoy the games.

Skill level

Friendly, sociable tennis for intermediate and advanced players

New to tennis? You're very welcome once you've had a few lessons and feel comfortable with the basics. Come and join us then.

Getting involved

How it works

1

Join the WhatsApp group

Request access via the link or QR code below. All coordination happens on WhatsApp.

2

£10 ball contribution

A one-off contribution that covers new tennis balls every few months. We get through a lot.

3

Turn up and play

Bring your racket, introduce yourself, and we'll pair you up straight away.

Skill level: we welcome intermediate to advanced players. Beginners are advised to get some lessons first to feel comfortable with the basics before requesting to join. Have a look at Local Tennis Coaching (We Do Tennis - Manchester Parks) for lessons in local parks.

Risk assessment: while the organisers conduct informal safety checks of the courts and equipment before each session, all participants play at their own risk and are responsible for their own personal safety.
